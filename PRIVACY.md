# ByteBouncer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

ByteBouncer is a desktop file-transfer application published by Sensei Lemon.

**What ByteBouncer collects:** nothing. ByteBouncer has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, accounts or crash reporting. We do not receive any information about you or your use of the app.

**Data stored on your device:** server addresses, user names, passwords and private-key paths you choose to save in the Site Manager, plus your settings and transfer log, are stored locally in your Windows user profile (`%APPDATA%\ByteBouncer`). They never leave your computer except to connect to the servers you specify. Uninstalling the app offers to delete this data.

**Network connections:** ByteBouncer connects only to the FTP, FTPS or SFTP servers you enter. Credentials are sent to those servers using the protocol you select; FTPS and SFTP encrypt them in transit, plain FTP does not. The optional "Open Proton VPN" button launches the Proton VPN application installed on your PC or opens protonvpn.com; ByteBouncer never handles VPN credentials.

**Children:** ByteBouncer is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** updates to this policy will be posted at this address with a new date.

**Contact:** the support contact shown on the Microsoft Store listing
